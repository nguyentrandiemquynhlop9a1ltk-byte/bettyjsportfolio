INFO

This project explores social issues within social media, focusing on three urgent concerns: cyberbullying, manipulation, and addiction. These problems are translated into an immersive exhibition, where each “room” is represented by a mascot that embodies a specific cause. By making invisible digital behaviours, especially manipulation become visible and tangible, the project aims to raise awareness in a more engaging and accessible way. Framed as a symposium, it also encourages participants to reflect, discuss, and spread these messages beyond the exhibition space.
