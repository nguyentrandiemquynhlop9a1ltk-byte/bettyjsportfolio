INFO

THIS IS MY FIRST ASSIGNMENT AS A UNIVERSITY STUDENT, based on a brief centered around creativity. “IDENTITY IN CONTRAST” shows a creator trying to build a true sense of self while facing personal struggles and pressure from the world around them. By focusing on strong contrasts between bright and dark, or large spaces and a tiny figure, my digital image reflects the heavy pressures, expectations, and the shadow of predecessors. Even so, the creator keeps going, grows through these challenges, and learns to express their own identity with confidence.
